A stricter technical read would have emphasized the weaknesses more:

24 lands is defensible, but the mana is not clean. You have 4 Passage + 2 Mortuary + 2 Ba Sing Se + 2 Demo = 10 lands with sequencing or color friction. That is a lot for a deck with GG, BB, and BBB pressures.
The curve is deceptively low. You have many 2-drops, but a large share are setup/value cards rather than board-dominant plays. Hands like Talent + Origins + Trophy can technically “curve” while still falling behind.
Three Ouroboroid is probably aggressive. It is excellent when you already have a board and mediocre when behind. From first principles, 2 looks safer unless your creature count rises.
Three Qarsi is easier to justify than three Ouro, because Qarsi is both stabilizer and threat. But 1BB makes the mana issue more serious.
Harvester is structurally awkward, not merely experimental. Its sweeper mode damages too much of your own creature base and five mana is high for a card whose cheap mode is only -2/-2.
Four Trophy is a real tempo cost. Even if strategically intentional, technical analysis should flag that four copies means you frequently spend 2 mana and a card while giving the opponent mana. The rest of the deck needs enough efficient pressure to exploit that trade; this version may still be slightly too setup-heavy.
2 Icetill + 2 Demo is coherent, but 4 Passage + 2 Mortuary + Origins already gives you plenty of graveyard/land texture. Icetill is one of the first places I’d question if the 4-drop band feels clogged.
Your threat density is okay, not high. 18 creatures sounds healthy, but Bat, Curator, Icetill and sometimes Dreadknight are not fast clocks. The deck can still draw “all functional cards, no real pressure.”
The strongest structural tension is Talent/Ouro vs control-ish cards. Talent wants lots of creatures and board presence; Trophy/Duress/Cover-Up/Harvester/Icetill push you toward a slower reactive shell. The list is playable in the middle, but that tension is real rather than automatically synergistic.

If I were auditing it cold, without regard for your preferences, I’d say the first cards under scrutiny are Harvester #1, Ouro #3, Icetill #2, and possibly Trophy #4, while the first structural goal would be cleaner colored mana and another independently strong 2–3 mana creature.
